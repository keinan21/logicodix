## Purpose

Explore how participants judge environmental claims, explain their reasoning, request evidence, and understand the proposed inspection step. Observe confusion around judgment choices and confidence questions. This is formative discovery, not an AI-feedback comparison or an effectiveness evaluation (§§02, 13; D-17).

## Participants and permissions

- Recruitment plan: 3–5 learners plus one teacher interviewed separately (§02). Record actual participation later, never as an achieved count here.
- Apply D-15: mark SMP participants `target`; SMA/college participants `proxy`. Record coarse education/age band, not birth date. Label any other non-target recruitment explicitly; do not pool proxies with target-user observations.
- Use `S1`, `S2`, etc. for learners; `G1` for the teacher. G1 supplies teacher context, not learner-performance evidence.
- Before any under-18 session, check written parent/teacher permission under D-15. Explain the task, voluntary participation, ability to skip/stop, and anonymous note-taking; obtain the participant's agreement. If permission or agreement is absent, do not begin.
- Keep permission records separate from coded notes. No names, schools, contact details, photos, or audio in notes. Paraphrase identifying details in volunteered explanations; otherwise preserve the participant's wording. Codes alone do not guarantee anonymity.
- [OPEN] Consent-record custodian, access, retention/deletion, and permission wording must be resolved before collecting data. No approval here for logging real student text, uploading it to AI, or committing notes/consent to the repository; see §09 and Open decisions.

## Learner interview — 10-minute target

Permission checks and card preparation happen before the timed session. Timing is a facilitation budget, never a speed score. Record overruns or skipped prompts; do not rush a learner into a judgment.

| Time | Facilitator script/action |
|---|---|
| 0:00–1:00 | “Ini bukan ujian. Kami ingin tahu cara kamu menilai informasi. Jawaban salah tidak masalah. Nama tidak dicatat. Kamu boleh melewati pertanyaan atau berhenti kapan saja. Bersedia lanjut?” Do not disclose the true/false mix. |
| 1:00–7:00 | Six cards, roughly one minute each, in recorded randomized order. “Kamu pernah lihat yang seperti ini?” Ask “Menurutmu benar atau salah?” using the two-option default (D-17); accept skipping without forcing a guess. “Apa yang bikin kamu bilang begitu?” Then “Informasi apa lagi yang kamu butuhkan buat lebih yakin?” Optional confidence prompt: “Seberapa yakin, 1 (tebak) sampai 5 (sangat yakin)?” Its placement remains [OPEN] until resolved; note whether asked or skipped. |
| 7:00–9:00 | After all card responses, reveal only user-verified keys with the checked source and explanation. “Ada yang mengejutkan?” Do not reveal answers between cards or coach earlier responses. |
| 9:00–10:00 | “Bagian mana yang membingungkan?” “Sebelum menilai klaim dalam game, apa yang ingin kamu cek dulu?” “Kalau ini dibuat jadi game balapan, bagian mana terasa seru atau membosankan?” “Biasanya kamu cek dari mana kalau ragu?” Thank participant; note any unanswered prompts. |

Three-option probe (D-17): optional, after baseline responses, without replacing them. Exact wording, timing relative to reveal, and which card/task to use are [OPEN]. Record the probe separately if approved; do not treat post-reveal responses as independent judgments or a fair two-vs-three comparison. If included, record extra time rather than silently compressing the core script.

The inspection question elicits expectations only. Without an actual evidence-inspection task, this protocol cannot establish usability of the game's inspect step.

## Known limits

- Tiny qualitative convenience sample; no population estimates, causal inference, or effectiveness claims. Recruitment and proxy mismatch limit transfer to SMP.
- No gameplay, AI-feedback, learning-transfer, or speed/scoring test occurs here. Preferences about racing do not establish learning benefit.
- The referenced classroom study of *Bad News* used upper-secondary students, not SMP (user-reported). [OPEN] Citation, URL, and methods have not been supplied or checked; do not treat it as evidence of effectiveness for SMP.
- All six source/key checks remain pending. K4's key is explicitly provisional; K6 lacks a source URL. Technical wording and dated/context-dependent claims can confound reasoning with reading difficulty or background knowledge.
- The kit's intended four-false/two-supported mix is not balanced; acquiescence or “all hoax” responses are possible. No insufficient-evidence card is supplied. A hypothetical third-option probe cannot validate that category.
- Confidence is self-report, not demonstrated skill; timing can affect responses. Facilitator prompts, repeat exposure, and answer reveal can influence later comments. No fair comparison is implied by a within-session probe.
- An in-class form would change the method; interview observations cannot be claimed for it. Format selection, inspection-task design, and final judgment/confidence UI remain [OPEN].
